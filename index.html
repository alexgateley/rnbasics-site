Questions

Are these the real NCLEX questions?

No. Every question and case study is original, written to the NCSBN test plan and clinical judgment model, then checked for accuracy and for the "tells" that let test-wise students guess. RN Basics is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with the NCSBN.

How does the adaptive mock exam work?

Each answer changes the difficulty of the next item, the way computerized adaptive testing does on the real exam. It gives between 85 and 150 items in up to 5 hours and stops once it is 95% confident your ability is above or below the passing standard. Three case studies are worked into the first 85 items.

Do I need an account? Where is my progress kept?

No account. Your progress stays in your browser on this device, and you can download a backup file and restore it on another device. The only data sent is anonymous usage analytics, which you can turn off in Settings → Your Data.

When are the iPhone and Android apps coming?

Both are coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Until then, the web app has everything and works in any browser, including on your phone.

What does the readiness estimate mean?

It blends your mock exam results, practice accuracy and case study scores, and shows how much data it is based on. It is an estimate of current performance to guide your studying, not a prediction that you will pass.

I think a question is wrong.

Tap "Report a problem with this question" under any explanation. It opens an email with the question already filled in, and every report is reviewed.
